The hall

NO. 02 — WHAT'S IN A NAME

Hymytalli translates roughly as "smile hall". The name is a promise kept simple: a place where the work is done properly, so the drive home is the good part.

The garage sits on Avaruuskatu in Haukilahti, the Westerås side of southern Espoo, a few minutes from Länsiväylä. It is small on purpose. One hall, a short list of jobs taken at a time, and the same hands on your car from start to finish.

Some cars here are daily drivers that deserve better than the quick-lube treatment. Some are weekend cars, classics, or long-term projects. All of them get the same thing: time, light, and attention.
